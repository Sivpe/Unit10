The skill · Understand paraphrasing

💡 What is paraphrasing?

ORIGINAL: "The shoes were too expensive for me."
➜
PARAPHRASE: "I couldn't afford the shoes."

Paraphrase = same meaning, different words. Tests often ask you to match a paraphrase to the original idea.

The paraphrase toolbox

🧰 HOW TO understand paraphrasing

① Read all the texts quickly for the main ideas.

② Look for synonyms — words that mean the same (comfortable ↦ comfy) & definitions.

③ Look for word families — comfort → comfortable.

④ Look for 'category' words for specific items — jewelry → bracelet.

Practice the skill

🧠 Try it: find the paraphrase

ORIGINAL (B): "Sneakers were so comfortable that people started to wear them as everyday footwear."
➜
PARAPHRASE: "Because sneakers were comfy, people began wearing them every day."

🤔 Which is the paraphrase? "People use sneakers all the time because they feel good." — YES! Same idea, different words.

Two more tools

🧩 Word families & category words

Word family
comfort → comfortable, uncomfortable
fashion → fashionable
style → stylish
Category
FOOTWEAR → sneakers, sandals, heels, boots
JEWELRY → bracelet, necklace, ring

👉 On tests, a category word in one text and a specific item in another is often the match.
