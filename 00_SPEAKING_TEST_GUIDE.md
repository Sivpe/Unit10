# 🗣 PAIR SPEAKING TEST — TEACHER GUIDE (Option A)
### Unit 10 · Changing Fashions · Go Beyond 3 · Secondary 8 · 2–3 min per pair

**How Option A works:** the pair speaks together on ONE topic (you observe + grade the interaction),
then **each student** does a short **solo AI speaking check on their own phone**. The AI gives an objective
score for fluency / pronunciation / grammar, and you combine it with your pair-interaction score.

> ✅ **Why Option A is best:** AI tools reliably grade **one voice at a time**. Grading a 2-person dialogue
> automatically is unreliable. So we let AI score each individual, and *you* judge how well they work as a pair.

---

## 1) THE CLASS ROUTINE (~45 min for a class of ~20 = 10 pairs)

| Step | Time | What happens |
|------|------|--------------|
| 1. Pair up + heads-up | 3 min | Show `PAIR_SPEAKING_TEST.html`. Explain the 3 parts (warm-up, pair task, solo AI check). Pairs choose Task 1 or Task 2. |
| 2. Prep silently | 2 min | Each student reads their **OWN role card** (A reads Student A, B reads Student B). |
| 3. Warm-up (all at once) | 30 s | Everyone says hi + 1 favourite clothing item. |
| 4. Pair talk (staggered) | 2–3 min / pair | One pair speaks at a time at the front, OR all pairs talk at once while you walk around. Use the on-screen **timer**. |
| 5. You score the pair | while they speak | Sit with the pair, fill the **Pair-interaction rubric** (see §3). |
| 6. Solo AI check | 2–3 min / student | Immediately after, each student does their own AI speaking check on their phone (§2). |
| 7. Combine + record | — | Put both scores in the **Google Sheets tracker** (§4) → one final mark. |

---

## 2) SET UP THE AI SPEAKING CHECK (student phones)

Pick one as your main grading tool. All work in a browser / app on a phone.

| Tool | What it gives you | How students use it |
|------|-------------------|---------------------|
| ⭐ **SmallTalk2Me** (smalltalk2.me) | CEFR level (A1–C2) + fluency, pronunciation, vocab, grammar | Free 15-min test — use this for a thorough solo score. |
| **Gliglish** (gliglish.com) | Real-time conversation feedback | No signup — best for the 2–3 min **purpose-built** practice chat. |
| **SpeakShark** (speakshark.com) | pronunciation + grammar + fluency + vocab score | Free sessions/day; gives a number you can record. |
| **ELSA Speak** (elsaspeak.com) | Phoneme-level **pronunciation** score | Use if pronunciation is your main focus. |

> ⚠️ **Note:** These are third-party services — check their privacy/age settings for your school's policy
> before rolling out. They also need internet + a working mic.

### The 2–3 minute solo AI prompt (paste into the AI app)
> *"Please have a short conversation with me in English. Ask me about my favourite clothes and fashion.
> Ask 5–6 questions about what I wear, my style, and how fashion has changed from the 1970s to today.
> Then grade me out of 20 for [see rubric below] and tell me what to improve."*

Students paste this once, then answer the AI's questions. For **SmallTalk2Me / ELSA**, they just use the
in-app speaking test directly.

---

## 3) THE RUBRIC — paste this into an AI evaluation tool (e.g. Snorkl) AND use it yourself

Copy everything in this block into the AI tool's criteria box (or print the table below).

```
Criteria for a 2–3 minute speaking task — Unit 10 "Changing Fashions" (CEFR A2–B1)

1. Task completion (content): Did they cover the task (ask questions, describe clothes, compare then/now)?
2. Vocabulary range: Did they use unit words - clothes, parts (collar, sleeve, pocket, belt),
   materials (denim, silk, cotton, leather, wool), look & fit (tight, baggy, loose, plain, patterned)?
3. Key verbs: Did they use fit, match, try on, wear, look good on correctly?
4. Grammar & accuracy: tenses, word order, articles - few mistakes for the level.
5. Pronunciation & fluency: clear words, good rhythm; not too many pauses or filler words (um, like).
6. Interaction (pair task): did they take turns, respond to their partner, ask follow-up questions?
7. Delivery: confidence, eye-contact, appropriate openings & closings.
```

### Score sheet — the same 7 criteria, /20

| # | Criterion | 0–1 | 2–3 | 4 |
|---|-----------|-----|-----|---|
| 1 | Task completion | little done | partly done | done well |
| 2 | Vocab range (unit words) | few | some | many + correct |
| 3 | Key verbs (fit/match/try on/wear/look good on) | none | some | all used correctly |
| 4 | Grammar & accuracy | many errors | some errors | few errors |
| 5 | Pronunciation & fluency | hard to follow | understandable | clear & smooth |
| 6 | Interaction (turn-taking, response) | one-sided | some back-and-forth | good conversation |
| 7 | Confidence & delivery | very shy | a little hesitant | confident |

**Total = ___ / 20.** (Each row 0–4, but treat rows 1–3 as worth double if you want /20 → /40,
or scale: 7 rows × 4 = 28 → convert to /20 by × 0.7.)

**Suggested grades:** 18–20 outstanding · 14–17 good · 10–13 developing · below 10 needs support.

---

## 4) GOOGLE SHEETS TRACKER (combining AI score + your pair score)

**Final mark formula:** `Final = (AI score + Pair-interaction score) ÷ 2`

1. In your Google Sheet add these column headers:
   `Name | Class | Partner | Task (1/2) | AI tool | AI score /20 | Pair score /20 | FINAL /20 | Teacher comment`

2. **Recommended format helper — paste this cell formula in the FINAL column** (adjust column letters to match):
   ```
   =ROUND(( E2 + F2 ) / 2, 1)
   ```
   where E2 = AI score cell and F2 = pair-interaction score cell.

3. **Bonus — color-code** with Conditional formatting: green if FINAL ≥ 14, yellow 10–13, red < 10.

---

## 5) WHAT TO PULL FROM THE STUDENT SIDE (to avoid cheating / keep it fair)
- Each student must speak at least **~50%** of the pair time.
- Record the pair talk on your phone (with permission) so you can re-listen if unsure — or compare
  the recording to the student's AI score to check consistency.
- The AI solo check uses a **different topic** than the pair task is fine, but keep it on the same
  unit theme so the vocabulary overlap is fair.

---

## 6) A SAMPLE "FINAL /20" SUMMARY
- **AI score (SmallTalk2Me/ELSA):** objective per-student (fluency, pron, grammar, vocab).
- **Pair score (teacher, rubric above):** interaction, turn-taking, task completion, confidence.
- **Final = average of the two.** Fair for both the strong-but-shy student and the fluent-but-monopolising one.
