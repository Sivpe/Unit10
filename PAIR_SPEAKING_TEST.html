🗣 Pair Speaking Test — Changing Fashions

Unit 10 · Go Beyond 3 · 2–3 minutes per pair · Mini speaking assessment

How How the test works

① Warm-up (30s): each student says their name and 1 favourite item of clothing.
② Main task (2–3 min): you speak with your partner using your role card.
③ After the pair talk: on your own phone, each of you does a 2–3 minute solo AI speaking check so the AI can score your fluency, pronunciation and grammar. Then the teacher combines both scores.

💡 Use the language of the unit: clothes, parts (collar, sleeve, pocket), materials (denim, silk, cotton, leather), and look & fit (tight, baggy, plain, patterned). Use the verbs fit · match · try on · wear · look good on.

Tasks Choose ONE task for your pair

🛍 Task 1 — "The fashion-shopping roleplay"

Scene: A teenager and a shop assistant in a clothes shop. They also talk about how styles have changed.

🕰 Task 2 — "Then and now"

Scene: Two friends look at an old family photo from the 1970s/1980s and compare the fashion then with today.

Roles Your role cards (one each)

🟦 STUDENT A

👥 Pair with Student B. Speak for 2–3 minutes. Use the unit vocabulary.

🟩 STUDENT B

👥 Pair with Student A. Speak for 2–3 minutes. Use the unit vocabulary.
