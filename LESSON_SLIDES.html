⏱ 0–5 min

🔥 Warm-up — "Guess the decade!"

Look at these 4 ideas. Shout the decade + one word to describe the clothes:

🕺
70s — bright, flared pants, big collars
🎸
80s — punk, leather, spiky hair
👖
90s — denim, hoodies, sneakers
🌱
2000s+ — eco-friendly, organic cotton

💬 Icebreaker: "What are you wearing right now? Compare with a photo of your parents!"

🧩 The 5 magic verbs

verbmeaningexample
fitbe the right sizeThese jeans fit me.
matchcolours go togetherMy scarf doesn't match my coat.
try ontest before buyingCan I try these shoes on?
wearhave on nowI always wear a scarf in winter.
look good onsuits a personThose sunglasses look good on you!

⏱ 70–85 min · Ex 8

🎤 Futurist fashion show — 2030s

In groups design tomorrow's outfit. Present it in 60 seconds:

  • Give it a name
  • Parts of clothes & materials
  • Look & fit (tight, baggy, patterned?)
  • Accessories

🏆 Vote for the best + most eco-friendly design.

⏱ 85–90 min

✍️ Exit ticket & homework

Which decade fits your style best, and why?

Write one sentence before you leave.

📚 Homework: find a photo of your parents'/grandparents' style and write 3 sentences about it — or do "Words & Beyond", p. 139 (Workbook p. 112).

Extension activity · Early finishers / to stretch strong students

🧠 Stretch 1 — "Upgrade your outfit"

Take a basic sentence and upgrade it with 2–3 words from today's vocabulary:

Basic: "The man is wearing a shirt."

Upgraded: "The man is wearing a patterned shirt with a big collar made of denim."

🎯 Challenge: try to use all 5 verbs (fit, match, try on, wear, look good on) in ONE short story about going shopping. Who can fit all five in?

Extension activity · Creative / group

🎨 Stretch 2 — "Redesign the school uniform"

Your uniform is out of fashion! In pairs, redesign it.

  • Pick a material (denim? cotton? wool?) and a fit (tight, baggy, loose).
  • Add parts: collar, cuffs, pockets, belt.
  • Decide patterned or plain, and bright or dark.

💬 Then present with "I think… because…". Vote for the best design and the most practical one.

Extension activity · Whole-class game · 5–10 min

🎮 Stretch 3 — "Fashion Pictionary / Charades"

Teams of 4. One student mimes or draws a word from today's list — the team shouts the answer.

🧥

🕶

⏳

👉 Rules: no speaking while miming! 60 seconds per word, one point each. Say the word and its category for a bonus point.

Extension activity · Writing / homework option

✍️ Stretch 4 — "Decade designer" postcard

Choose your favourite decade (70s, 80s, 90s or 2000s). Write a postcard to a friend describing the fashion then.

"Dear Mara,
Here in the 1970s, everyone wears bright clothes and flared pants with big collars. I'm wearing a patterned shirt and platform shoes. It's really fun!
Love, Dara"

🎯 Use at least 6 vocabulary words and the correct tenses.

Extension activity · Critical thinking / speaking

🌍 Stretch 5 — "Eco-couture debate"

Fashion today is worried about the planet. Discuss in groups:

  • Why do people choose non-leather shoes and organic cotton?
  • Is fast fashion (cheap, new clothes every week) good or bad? Why?
  • What could be the "key word" for the 2030s? (e.g. recycled? smart? shared?)

👉 Sentence starter: "I think the 2030s will be a decade of ______ because ______."
