THE 1970s

In the 1970s, clothes were bright and colourful. Men let their hair grow long and wore shirts with huge collars turned up. Women wore patterned shirts with long sleeves and plain pants. Pants had waists high above the waist, were tight at the top but wide below the knees — the famous bell-bottom look. People finished the look with sandals and platform shoes.

THE 1980s

In the 1980s, fashion became more extreme. People loved bright colours and lots of accessories. Women wore baggy wool sweaters and shoulder pads. Some young people followed the punk look: short spiky hair, leather jackets, and jeans.

THE 1990s

In the 1990s, denim became the most popular material. Everyone wore jeans and T-shirts with short and long sleeves — plus sneakers and hoodies. Hair became smooth and straight, and dark colours replaced the bright colours of the 1970s. The look was casual: jeans, hoodies, and flannel shirts.

THE 2000s & THE PRESENT

In the 2000s and today, people became more eco-friendly. They chose non-leather purses and shoes, and organic cotton started to appear in major department stores. Everybody — from babies to grandparents — wears jeans. Elegant patterned shirts and soft silk scarves are combined with jeans and sneakers. Fashion today mixes comfort with care for the planet.
