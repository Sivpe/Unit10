📖 Reading text — "The Vintage Shop" (read before Q11–Q22)

Sokha loves fashion, but not the new clothes in the mall. He goes to a small shop that sells old clothes — a vintage shop.

The owner, Uncle Dara, takes a red jacket off the shelf. "This is from the 1970s," he says. In the 1970s, people wore bright colours and wide, flared trousers. The red jacket has a very large collar and it is made of thick cotton.

"And this green coat is from the 1980s," he says. In the 1980s, fashion was louder. Young people wore leather jackets and shiny metal. This green coat has shoulder pads that make the shoulders look very wide.

"This pair of blue jeans is from the 1990s," Uncle Dara says. In the 1990s, jeans were the most popular clothes in the world. They were simple and comfortable. Everyone — from children to grandparents — wore jeans every day.

Today, Sokha cares about nature. He likes clothes made of organic cotton because they are good for the planet. He buys old clothes instead of new ones. "New fashion is always expensive," he says. "I can look stylish and help the Earth at the same time."

Uncle Dara smiles. "Fashion changes every year," he says, "but a good jacket never goes out of style."
